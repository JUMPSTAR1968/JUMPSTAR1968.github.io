JEDIDIAH LOPEZ

Also known as JUMPSTAR1968, Jedidiah Lopez is a Game Developer, Music Producer, and Content Creator whose interests span video games, music, sports, performing arts, and storytelling.

His creative pursuits include game development, graphic design, video editing, hosting, voice-over work, narration, and keyboard performance. Through these activities, he continues to explore different ways of expressing his ideas, developing his abilities, and connecting with people.

As a developer, he aspires to create engaging and memorable gaming experiences. As a performer, he enjoys entertaining audiences through music, hosting, and live performances. As a content creator, he expresses his creativity through visual design, digital media, and original projects.

Driven by his passion for creativity and entertainment, he believes in using his talents, abilities, and dedication to bring happiness to others through the work he creates and the experiences he shares.
